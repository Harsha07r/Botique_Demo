# Photos still needed

No photographs exist in this repository: the site shows drawn colour placeholders (labelled "Photo needed")
until real photos are uploaded in the CMS. Nothing below can be generated or stolen from elsewhere; it has to be
shot in the shop.

## Shooting rules (same for every photo)

- **Product only.** No models, faces, hands or mannequins.
- **Same plain backdrop** (off-white or light grey cloth or paper) and **soft, even daylight** (a window with a
  white curtain, or two diffused lights). No flash, no filters, no colour edits beyond exposure.
- **4:5 portrait**, 1600 x 2000 px (the CMS rejects other ratios).
- **True colours.** Shoot next to a grey card if possible. Check the photo against the real cloth on a phone.
- File format: JPG or PNG straight from the camera/phone. Do not upload WebP; the site converts automatically.

## Per product: 3 required, 5 recommended

The CMS will not let a product be published with fewer than the first three.

| # | Slot | What to show | Required |
|---|------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | Full piece | Whole piece, neatly folded or draped flat, border and pallu visible | Yes |
| 2 | Pallu | The pallu end (for kurta sets and lehengas: the dupatta end) | Yes |
| 3 | Border | Close view of the border and where it meets the body | Yes |
| 4 | Blouse piece | The blouse piece (kurta set: the yoke; lehenga: the blouse) | Recommended |
| 5 | Texture close-up | Macro of the weave or embroidery, about 10 cm across | Recommended |
| 6 | Extra view | Optional: anything else worth showing | Optional |

## Site photos (all in the CMS, none required to launch, all strongly recommended)

| Where | What | Size |
|-------|------|------|
| Home page, hero image | One of your best pieces, product-only, calm background | 1600 x 2000 (4:5) or 1:1 |
| Home page, 6 occasion tiles | Wedding, Festive, Party, Puja, Office, Everyday: one piece per tile | 4:5 |
| Shop details, real shop photo | The shop front or interior. A real photo of your shop is what builds trust; people and the owner may appear here | 4:3, at least 1600 px wide |
| About page photo | Optional: the shop, or the stock shelves | 4:3 |

## Other content the owner must supply (not photos)

- WhatsApp number and phone number (Shop details). Until set, WhatsApp opens its contact picker.
- Real opening hours, full address and a Google Maps search text.
- 3 to 6 real customer reviews with permission (the one on the site now is a labelled placeholder).
- Real prices, availability and fabric details for every product. The 10 products in this repo are a sample
  catalogue written to test the site; confirm or correct each one against actual stock before publishing.
- Instagram handle, and the final delivery and return terms (the wording on the Policies page is a draft).
